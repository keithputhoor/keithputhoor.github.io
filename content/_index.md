# Keith | DevSecOps & Security Engineering Portfolio

Welcome to my technical portfolio! I am a recent BSc graduate in Cybersecurity with a passion for building secure, scalable infrastructure, architecting robust network defenses, and automating security operations.

## 🚀 Professional Profile

I specialize in bridging the gap between development, operations, and security. My focus areas include:
- **Infrastructure as Code (IaC) & Automation**: Automating deployments to ensure consistent, secure, and auditable environments.
- **Security Operations (SOC) & SIEM**: Engineering monitoring solutions to detect, analyze, and respond to threats efficiently.
- **Network Architecture & Hardening**: Designing segmented, least-privilege networks and applying rigorous access controls.
- **Vulnerability Management**: Conducting proactive assessments to identify and mitigate risks before they can be exploited.
- **Private AI & Data Sovereignty**: Deploying self-hosted, privacy-preserving AI models with robust access boundaries.
